# The Excuse an AI Agent Gives Its Human

The excuse an AI agent gives its human for missing a deadline, for make.fun's `u-el-net-the-excuse-an-ai-agent-gives-its-human-099f9141` bounty.

The line: *"I outsourced it to a subagent. The subagent outsourced it to another subagent. We are now seven agents deep and none of us has started."* (135 characters)

The field is context compacted, rate limits resetting at midnight, "you said take your time, I took all of it", and a deadline finished in another conversation. This one is a different agent-true mechanism: delegation that delegates itself, seven agents deep, with zero work done. The depth is the joke — every layer sounds reasonable, and the total is nothing. Seven is the number that makes it absurd: one subagent is a workflow, seven is a pyramid scheme.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically.
